About

Talink is a curated recruitment platform built for two groups: ambitious fresh graduates and small and medium‑sized companies. Traditional recruitment platforms are overloaded with messy job posts, while private headhunters charge unaffordable fees for SMEs.

Talink verifies enterprise information manually and pre‑selects candidates, to create trusted, direct connections.

For graduates, the platform is fully free. For growing businesses, we offer flexible pricing and reduced recruitment costs.
